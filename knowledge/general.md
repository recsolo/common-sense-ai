# Commonsense Knowledge Base — General

## Weather
rain → bring an umbrella
cold weather → wear a jacket
snow → drive carefully
hot weather → stay hydrated
storm warning → reschedule outdoor plans

## Sports (generic)
losing record → fans are frustrated
winning streak → fans are excited
playoff time → bigger audience, more engagement
game day → people are watching / distracted
offseason → lower sports engagement

## Time of day
morning → people check email / news
lunchtime → people are on a break, good for quick posts
evening → people wind down, good for casual content
late night → smaller audience, more personal tone

## Days of week
monday → back to work energy
friday → weekend anticipation
weekend → casual / personal content performs better
sunday evening → people prep for the week

## Food & cooking
grocery store → buying ingredients
recipe → cooking at home
leftovers → meal from yesterday
takeout → not cooking tonight

## Health & fitness
gym → workout session
running → cardio / outdoor exercise
rest day → recovery, not working out
sore → recovering from exercise

## Social
birthday → celebrate / send wishes
anniversary → celebrate milestone
meeting → work / professional context
travel → away from home / different timezone

## Technology
update available → install when convenient
slow connection → use lighter content
battery low → charge device soon
