# Commonsense Knowledge Base — Sports

## NFL / Football
game day → post before kickoff for best reach
blowout loss → frustrated tone, low engagement
close loss → fans still engaged, more discussion
big win → high engagement, celebratory tone works
bye week → no game this week
playoffs → higher stakes, bigger audience
Super Bowl → biggest audience of the year

## NBA / Basketball
game night → evening tip-off, fans tuning in after work
trade deadline → roster changes, speculation high
finals → highest stakes, national audience

## MLB / Baseball
opening day → season starts, fans excited
playoffs → October baseball / higher stakes
world series → biggest audience of the season

## Soccer
matchday → fans tuned in worldwide
derby → local rivalry, emotions run high
world cup → global audience, highest stakes

## General tone guide
win → celebratory, excited, proud
loss → frustrated, analytical
close game → dramatic, entertaining
injury news → concerned, respectful
trade news → analytical, speculative
